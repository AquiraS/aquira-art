ACCESSIBILITY

So that everyone can read with ease.

Aquira values making works and information accessible to as many people as possible, regardless of device or environment.

DISPLAY SETTINGS

Adjustments available on this site

Text and contrast

From Display settings at the lower right of the screen, you can choose text size, high contrast, and link underlining. Your settings are saved in your browser.

Reduce motion

Choosing reduced motion minimizes on-screen animation and transition movement. The site also respects your device’s reduced-motion setting.

OUR COMMITMENT

What we continue to improve

Keyboard access and structure

We check that key information and controls can be reached by keyboard alone, and that headings, links, and landmarks communicate the structure of the content.

Alternatives to visual information

Meaningful images include alternative text, while purely decorative images are excluded from screen-reader output. Where moving image is published, captions or transcripts are considered according to the content.

Review and contact

If you notice a problem or have a suggestion, please contact us with the circumstances, device or browser, and page URL. We will review the matter and respond within reasonable limits.
