OFFICIAL ARTIST PROFILE

About Aquira

Aquira is a Yokohama-based photographer, audiovisual artist, and contemporary artist. Through photography, moving image, and digital expression, the practice considers personal memory, the city, place, and relationships with society.

Fields of expression

Beginning with photography, Aquira has extended the practice into moving image, audiovisual expression, and contemporary art. Working across media, the work reconfigures sensations held in light, time, and place.

Relationship with society

Aquira’s activity extends beyond the presentation of work. Through spaces for dialogue and learning, the practice explores how expression might create new points of contact between people and society.
